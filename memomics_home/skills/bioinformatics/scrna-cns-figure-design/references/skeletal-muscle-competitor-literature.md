## Strategic takeaway for F2

If F2 = "L3 annotation + composition change + proportion boxplots + marker dotplot", a reviewer
who knows Moreno-Justicia (Nat Commun 2025) and Gong (Aging Cell 2026) will dismiss it as
redundant. The differentiation must be the **five-effect systematic comparison**
(aging / T2D / Ex-Young / Ex-Old / Ex-T2D effect matrix + reversibility axes) — the competitor
papers do NOT have a paired multi-condition exercise design. That is the publishable increment.
