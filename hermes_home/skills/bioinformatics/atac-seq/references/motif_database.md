## Key TF Motifs for Skeletal Muscle Aging

### Muscle Fiber Type
- MAF, MAFB (fast fiber master regulator)
- MYOD1, MYOG (myogenic)
- MEF2A, MEF2C, MEF2D (fiber type specification)
- TEAD1, TEAD4 (TEA domain)

### Atrophy
- FOXO1, FOXO3 (atrophy master regulators)
- MYOG (denervation-induced)
- RUNX1 (protective in denervation)
- STAT3 (cachexia)

### Mitochondria
- PPARG, ESRRA, NRF1, TFAM

### Senescence
- E2F, DP1, HMGA, P53, NFKB
